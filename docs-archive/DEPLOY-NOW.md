# 🚀 Deploy Your App to Cloud RIGHT NOW!

## ⚡ Super Quick Instructions (2 minutes to read, 5 minutes to deploy)

### Step 1: Go Here
👉 **https://railway.app** 👈

### Step 2: Login
Click **"Login with GitHub"**

### Step 3: Deploy
1. Click **"New Project"**
2. Click **"Deploy from GitHub repo"**
3. Select **`ForeningsForsaljning`**
4. Railway starts deploying automatically! ⚡

### Step 4: Wait
⏳ Watch the build logs (about 2-3 minutes)

You'll see:
- ✅ Installing dependencies...
- ✅ Building application...
- ✅ Starting server...
- ✅ Deployment successful!

### Step 5: Get Your URL
1. Click **"Settings"** tab
2. Scroll to **"Domains"**
3. Click **"Generate Domain"**
4. **Copy your URL:** `https://yourapp.up.railway.app`
